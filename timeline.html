Timeline & Expert Estimates

Key events in Bitcoin's quantum journey and expert predictions for when quantum computers might pose a real threat.

Timeline of Key Events

1994
Shor's Algorithm Published
Peter Shor shows quantum computers could break RSA and ECC. The theoretical threat to public-key crypto begins. read more
2009
Bitcoin Launches
Satoshi uses ECDSA (secp256k1). Early coins use P2PK — public keys directly exposed on-chain. read more
2019
Google Claims Quantum Supremacy
53-qubit Sycamore processor completes a specific task in 200 seconds that would take classical supercomputers ~10,000 years. Not relevant to cryptography, but a milestone. read more
2021
Taproot Activates on Bitcoin
P2TR addresses expose a tweaked public key, making them vulnerable to long exposure quantum attacks — a concern noted by researchers. read more
Aug 2024
NIST Finalizes Post-Quantum Standards
FIPS 203 (ML-KEM), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA), FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA) — first official post-quantum cryptography standards. Ready for use. read more
Dec 2024
BIP-360: Pay-to-Merkle-Root (P2MR) Proposed
Hunter Beast, Ethan Heilman, Isabel Foxen Duke propose P2MR — a new output type resistant to long exposure quantum attacks. Uses bc1z addresses. Status: Draft. read more
Dec 2024
Google Willow Chip
105 qubits with breakthrough error correction — errors decrease as more qubits are added (below threshold). Still far from cryptographic relevance. read more
Feb 2025
Microsoft Majorana 1 Chip
World's first topological qubit chip published in Nature. 8 topological qubits using topoconductors. Claims clear path to 1M qubits on palm-sized chip. Selected for DARPA US2QC final phase. read more
July 2025
Legacy Signature Sunset BIP Proposed
Jameson Lopp and Christian Papathanasiou propose phasing out legacy ECDSA/Schnorr signatures and eventually freezing quantum-vulnerable UTXOs (~25% of all Bitcoin outputs). The most aggressive quantum defense proposal yet. read more
Oct 2025
Google Quantum Echoes
Published in Nature. First verifiable quantum advantage algorithm on hardware. Willow chip ran OTOC algorithm 13,000× faster than fastest classical supercomputer for molecular structure analysis. read more
2025–2026
DARPA Quantum Benchmarking Initiative
US government actively investing in utility-scale fault-tolerant quantum computing. Microsoft selected as one of two companies in final phase for US2QC program. read more
Sept 2025
Caltech 6,100-Qubit Neutral Atom Array
Record-breaking 6,100 neutral atom qubits in a single array, operating at room temperature with 99.98% accuracy and 12.6-second coherence times. Published in Nature. read more
Feb 2026
BlackRock Flags Quantum Risk in ETF Filing
BlackRock updated its iShares Bitcoin ETF (IBIT) registration statement to flag quantum computing as a potential risk to the Bitcoin network's integrity. Capriole's Charles Edwards argues quantum risk should be priced into BTC until resolved. read more

Delft University and ICMM-CSIC achieve first real-time readout of Majorana topological qubits using quantum capacitance. Solves the key obstacle to using topological qubits for computation. Parity coherence exceeds 1 millisecond. read more

Steel rises at PsiQuantum's Chicago South Works campus — the world's first utility-scale quantum computing facility targeting 1 million photonic qubits. BlackRock-backed. Reignites Bitcoin quantum security debate, though 1M physical qubits remains far from cryptographic relevance. read more

Google's Quantum AI team publishes whitepaper showing Bitcoin's cryptography could fall to fewer than 500,000 physical qubits — far below the "millions" often cited. Mar 2026

Jonas Nick announces SHRIMPS — a breakthrough in hash-based post-quantum signatures. Combines SHRINCS (~324B single-device) with multi-device support: primary device signs at ~324 bytes, backup devices at ~2.5KB, all from the same static seed backup. 3× smaller than SLH-DSA. Published on Delving Bitcoin.

Caltech and Oratomic publish new error correction architecture for neutral atom platforms that reduces physical-to-logical qubit ratio from ~1,000:1 to ~5:1. Brian Armstrong takes personal oversight of Bitcoin's post-quantum transition, forming a coalition with Block, TBD, and Bitcoin Core contributors. Active discussion on bitcoin-dev mailing list, bitcointalk, and X/Twitter. Key debates: signature size tradeoffs, urgency vs. caution, Taproot vulnerability. Status: Draft v0.11.0. read more

Potential future BIP for PQ signatures (SPHINCS+, ML-DSA) via OP_SUCCESSx upgrade path. Would protect against short exposure attacks too. Major challenge: signature sizes (8–50KB vs 64 bytes for Schnorr).

US government mandates software/networking upgraded to post-quantum schemes. Signal for urgency across all crypto systems.

2035–2050+?
Cryptographically Relevant Quantum Computer?
Estimates vary wildly. Most researchers: 15-30+ years. Some governments plan for 10 years. Microsoft's topological approach could be a wildcard. Nobody knows for sure.

Reality: Timeline predictions have a terrible track record. Google's quantum supremacy came faster than expected; IBM's quantum advantage promises have been slower. The only certainty is uncertainty. Bitcoin must prepare for multiple scenarios.

Why Estimates Vary So Dramatically

The Optimists (2030-2035):
  • Point to exponential hardware improvements (Google Willow, Microsoft Majorana)
  • Government funding (DARPA US2QC, Chinese quantum programs)
  • Corporate roadmaps promising millions of qubits
  • National security agencies planning for worst-case scenarios
The Realists (2040-2050):
  • Current systems are orders of magnitude away from CRQC
  • Error correction, coherence, and connectivity remain unsolved
  • Physical qubits ≠ logical qubits (need ~1000:1 ratio)
  • Academic consensus based on current progress rates
The Skeptics (2050+):
  • Fundamental physics limits to quantum coherence
  • Classical computers also improving (resistance is not futile)
  • Quantum error correction may be intractable
  • Pattern of overpromising in quantum industry

Government and Policy Timelines

Regardless of technical realities, government mandates are creating real deadlines for post-quantum cryptography adoption:

2030
CNSA 2.0: Software & Networking
NSA requires all software and networking to use post-quantum cryptography. First major government deadline.
2033
CNSA 2.0: Operating Systems
All operating systems and web browsers must be fully post-quantum compliant for US government use.
2035
NIST: ECC Phase-Out
Elliptic curve cryptography disallowed in US federal systems (except as part of hybrid schemes). Bitcoin still relies entirely on ECC.
The Policy Paradox: Government agencies are mandating post-quantum cryptography transitions years before quantum computers are expected to threaten current systems. This creates pressure on all cryptographic systems, including Bitcoin, even if the technical threat is still distant.

What This Means for Bitcoin

The wide range of timeline estimates creates a strategic challenge for Bitcoin:

  • Too early: Implementing quantum resistance prematurely could introduce complexity and tradeoffs for a non-existent threat
  • Too late: Bitcoin consensus changes take years to implement — waiting until quantum computers are imminent may be too late
  • Just right: The Goldilocks zone requires constant monitoring and community consensus on risk vs. reward
The emerging consensus: Start preparing now with low-risk, reversible steps (like BIP-360) while continuing to monitor quantum developments. This allows Bitcoin to respond quickly if timelines compress while avoiding premature optimization if they don't.
