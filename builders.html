Who's Building Bitcoin Quantum Resistance

Companies and entities actively developing solutions to protect Bitcoin from quantum computing threats.

Bitcoin Core Developers

BIP-360 authors Hunter Beast, Ethan Heilman, and Isabel Foxen Duke are proposing Pay-to-Merkle-Root (P2MR) — the first concrete Bitcoin quantum defense merged into the official BIP repository.

→ BIP-360 Specification

Chaincode Labs

Published "Bitcoin and Quantum Computing: Current Status and Future Directions" paper. Leading cryptographers Tim Rufing, Jonas Nick, and Ethan Heilman are actively working on Bitcoin's quantum readiness.

→ Research Paper 𝕏

BTQ Technologies

First to demonstrate a quantum-safe Bitcoin implementation using NIST ML-DSA signatures. Bitcoin Quantum Core Release 0.2 protects the entire $2.4T Bitcoin market with production-ready code.

→ btq.com 𝕏

Blockstream Research

Led by Adam Back, working on hash-based signatures and post-quantum cryptographic solutions for Bitcoin. Contributing to quantum resistance research and BIP-360 development efforts.

→ blockstream.com 𝕏

Project 11 (P11)

Quantum computing research org behind the Q-Day Prize — offering 1 BTC to the first team to break an elliptic curve cryptographic (ECC) key using a quantum computer. Launched April 2025 to benchmark the real-world quantum threat to Bitcoin.

→ projecteleven.com 𝕏

Anduro

Multi-sidechain platform dedicated to Bitcoin quantum resistance. Features quantum-resistant bridging via MARA Slipstream and QR assets pegged 1:1 with Bitcoin. Chains use Bitcoin Proof-of-Work with various QR use cases.

→ anduro.io 𝕏

Independent Developers

Open-source developers building quantum resistance tools independently. Emiliano Solazzi built PQ-PSBT, a hybrid wallet layer that dual-signs transactions with both classical (Schnorr) and post-quantum (ML-DSA/Falcon) keys. 136 tests passing, 0.5ms overhead — quantum insurance you can use today.

Strategy

Largest corporate Bitcoin holder (~500K+ BTC). Actively researching post-quantum positioning to protect their multi-billion dollar Bitcoin treasury from future quantum threats.

→ strategy.com 𝕏

Coinbase

Major exchange and custodian exploring quantum-resistant custody solutions. As custodian for Bitcoin ETFs, quantum readiness is critical for institutional client trust.

→ coinbase.com 𝕏
