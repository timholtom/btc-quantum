BREAKING

Google: Breaking Bitcoin Requires Fewer Qubits Than Thought, Taproot Partly to Blame

Google's Quantum AI team publishes whitepaper showing Bitcoin's cryptography could be broken with fewer than 500,000 physical qubits — far below the "millions" often cited. Taproot's design exposes public keys by default, expanding the quantum attack surface. A mid-transaction attack model could derive private keys in ~9 minutes (before Bitcoin's ~10 min confirmation), giving attackers a 41% success rate. ~6.9M BTC (~⅓ of supply) already sits in addresses with exposed public keys. Google used zero-knowledge proofs for responsible disclosure.

CoinDesk: Google's 2029 PQC Deadline — Bitcoin Has No Response

CoinDesk reports that Google has set a formal 2029 corporate deadline to migrate all authentication services to post-quantum cryptography. The company warns quantum threats to digital signatures "require transition prior to a cryptographically relevant quantum computer." Android 17 already integrates post-quantum signatures; Chrome supports PQ key exchange. Bitcoin has no equivalent upgrade timeline. The contrast with Ethereum's coordinated multi-year response is stark.

NEW

ArXiv Paper: Quantum Mining Requires Kardashev Type II Energy — Physically Impossible

New academic paper (arXiv:2603.25519) provides the first end-to-end cost analysis of quantum Bitcoin mining via Grover's algorithm. At Bitcoin's current difficulty, a quantum mining fleet would require ~10²³ qubits and ~10²⁵ watts — approaching Kardashev Type II (entire star's output). Even at the most favorable settings, ~10⁸ qubits and ~10⁴ MW are needed (a national grid). Conclusively settles: SHA-256 mining is not a viable quantum attack vector. The real threat remains Shor's algorithm against ECDSA signatures.

CoinDesk: Diverging Quantum Strategies Across Bitcoin, Ethereum & Solana

Major CoinDesk analysis reveals how crypto's three largest ecosystems are taking vastly different approaches to quantum resistance. Bitcoin debates BIP-360 and the controversial "Hourglass" proposal (gradually restricting vulnerable UTXOs), while Ethereum leverages account abstraction for opt-in migration. Jefferies told investors to drop BTC over quantum risk; Ark Invest pushed back. ~$440B in vulnerable BTC at stake, including ~1M coins linked to Satoshi.

Ethereum Foundation Launches pq.ethereum.org — Dedicated PQ Research Hub

The Ethereum Foundation publishes pq.ethereum.org, consolidating 8+ years of post-quantum research into a public roadmap. Plans a multi-layer migration through seven incremental upgrades over ~4 years, replacing BLS signatures with hash-based alternatives (leanXMSS) and using account abstraction for opt-in wallet migration. L1 protocol upgrades target 2029.
