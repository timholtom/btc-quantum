NIST Post-Quantum Standards Finalized
August 2024 — The cryptographic building blocks are ready
Three quantum-proof encryption standards are now official (ML-KEM, ML-DSA, SLH-DSA). These are the replacement algorithms Bitcoin could adopt. The US government is already mandating their use by 2030–2035.
📝
BIP-360: Pay-to-Merkle-Root (P2MR) Drafted
December 2024 — First concrete Bitcoin proposal, currently in review
Creates new bc1z addresses that never expose your public key on-chain. Status: Draft v0.11.0, under community review.
🔧
PQ-PSBT Wallet Layer (Independent Dev)
February 2026 — Wallet-level quantum insurance, working today
Developer Emiliano Solazzi built a hybrid wallet that dual-signs transactions with both classical (Schnorr) and post-quantum (ML-DSA/Falcon) keys. 136 tests passing, 0.5ms overhead.
